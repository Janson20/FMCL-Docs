11. Music player notice ​

The music player borrows design ideas from the open-source project LX Music and uses a similar approach to obtaining data from third-party music platforms. The following clauses apply specifically to the music player.

Data sources

  1. Music search and online playback connect to the public interfaces of third-party platforms (including but not limited to Kuwo, Kugou, Migu, QQ Music and NetEase Cloud Music). The Software merely displays the returned data and is not responsible for its legality, accuracy or timeliness.
  2. The Software does not store, cache or distribute any music files. Online playback obtains a temporary playback link from the third-party platform only when you explicitly press play, and keeps no audio data afterwards.
  3. The accuracy of search results depends on what the third-party interfaces return; the Software cannot guarantee that results match the platform itself.
  4. The "original artist" marker on search results is derived by rule matching over metadata returned by the platform (artist, release date, album, cover references and so on); it is not a manually reviewed judgement. The Contributors are not responsible for the accuracy of platform data and do not guarantee the marker; it is for reference only, and where it differs from the actual original, the platform's own data and your own verification prevail.
  5. Automatic cross-source fallback: when the selected source cannot provide a complete playback link (for example VIP-only or unlicensed tracks) or returns only a preview clip, the Software automatically searches other sources, including Bilibili, by track name, artist and duration and plays the replacement. This matching is based on platform metadata rules and may match a cover, live version, instrumental or another version; the Software is not responsible for the accuracy of the match. The copyright of any replacement track belongs to its original rights holder.
  6. Bilibili risk-control verification: Bilibili may require a security check (slider CAPTCHA) for anonymous search requests. The Software then shows a prompt and opens your system browser so you can complete the check the platform requires. The verification process, content and result are entirely controlled by Bilibili; the temporary credential produced by verification is used only for the current search request, and the Software neither collects nor stores your verification information. If you decline to complete the verification, the fallback feature is unavailable, and nothing else is affected.

Copyright

  1. Copyright in music searched and played online through the Software belongs to the original rights holders, including but not limited to record labels, musicians and music platforms.
  2. The Software owns no copyright in any music data and does not store, copy or distribute it in any form.
  3. If you want to keep a track after previewing it, purchase it or obtain authorisation through lawful channels. The Software provides technical exploration and research only; you bear full legal responsibility for any copyright dispute arising from your use of this feature.

  2. You must use the online music features in compliance with local law. If local law prohibits obtaining music through unofficial channels, you must not use these features.
  3. If playback fails or search results look wrong because a third-party interface changed, broke or was blocked, the Contributors are not obliged to fix it.
  4. Tracks played through cross-source fallback may come from another platform; their availability and audio quality depend on the providing source and are not guaranteed. Search on platforms such as Bilibili is rate limited and protected by risk control, so a fallback may legitimately fail.

User responsibility

  1. Before installing any third-party plugin, review the permission list it requests and the risk level of each permission. Granting high-risk permissions such as executing external processes, raw socket connections or launch hooks requires careful consideration.
  2. Obtain .fmpl files from trusted channels. Plugins from unknown sources may contain malicious code.
  3. You bear the full risk of using plugin features. If you find a plugin with security problems or infringing behaviour, uninstall and delete it immediately.

Plugin management

  1. You can enable, disable or uninstall any plugin at any time in Settings → Plugins.
  2. Granted permissions can be revoked at any time on the plugin management page.
  3. When a plugin is disabled or uninstalled, the hooks and UI components it registered are removed automatically; the launcher keeps working normally.

Acceptance

By installing or enabling any plugin you confirm that you have read and fully accept the clauses above. If you do not agree, do not use the plugin system.
